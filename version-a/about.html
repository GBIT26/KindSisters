Our People

Our Story

Kind Sisters was created to strengthen community in the City of Stirling and surrounding areas since 2015.

Our team works to provide support networks and supply donations to those in need. This work could not be achieved without our fantastic volunteers and generous donors.

Meet the Kind Sisters Team

Jody Rynski

Director & Founder

Jody founded Kind Sisters to create a grassroots community of support for vulnerable women and children in the City of Stirling and surrounding areas. With a deep passion for social justice and community empowerment, Jody leads the team in delivering essential relief and connection programs that make a real difference.

Penny Webb

Director

Penny brings over 25 years of experience as a senior leader, Board Director, and strategic communicator across the private and not-for-profit sectors. She is the CEO at People Who Care and holds a Master of Business Administration and a Master of Business Leadership. She is deeply passionate about reducing inequality for women.

Alison Taylor

Director

Alison is committed to strengthening community and supporting women and families facing hardship. With experience across community services and volunteer coordination, Alison plays a key role in building partnerships and ensuring Kind Sisters delivers meaningful, lasting impact in the Perth community.
