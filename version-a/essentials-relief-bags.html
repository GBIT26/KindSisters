Essentials Relief Bags

We make staple, non-perishable food and hygiene products available to families in poverty through government primary schools in the Mirabooka area. Our Essentials Relief Bags address immediate needs to help put food on the table and support basic hygiene practices.

These bags are delivered directly into the hands of those in need through support workers in the schools. This is the key to accessibility as school staff know their families and can ensure that those who need it receive support in a discreet and respectful way. This approach breaks down barriers and improves quality of life for families as parents can meet their children's basic needs and send them to school ready to learn and socialise with their peers.

Our bags can include:

  • Non-Perishables such as rice, pasta, passata, tinned tuna, cereal, long-life milk, tinned fruit/vegetables, oil, chickpeas, muesli bars, flour, sugar, coffee.
  • Hygiene goods including tissues, soap, spray & wipe, toilet cleaner, toothpaste, toothbrushes, shampoo, tissues, deodorant, toilet paper, women's hygiene products, dishwashing liquid and laundry detergent.
