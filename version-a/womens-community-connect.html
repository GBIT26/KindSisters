Women's Community Connect Events

Women's Community Connect Events provide a safe, inclusive, and supportive space for vulnerable women from all walks of life and diverse cultural backgrounds—focusing on those experiencing poverty, social disadvantage, family violence, and resettlement challenges as refugees and low-income migrants—to learn, grow, connect and build community.

  • Compassionate community: An emotionally and culturally safe-space to build community connections while sharing morning tea.
  • Practical assistance: Personal care-packs, clothing and essentials.
  • Information/referrals: Connections to services, including housing, family violence, counselling, and emergency relief.
  • Capacity-building education: Workshops to build skills and confidence to reach their full potential.
  • Therapeutic or Skill building activities: Capturing stories/experiences to facilitate the healing process.
